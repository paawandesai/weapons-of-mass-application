`src/demo/`: `factory.ts` is the port factory that, per tenant flag `tenants.demo`, swaps Gmail, Calendar and the inbox reader for the `demo_mailbox_messages` and `demo_calendar_events` tables; `recruiter.ts` is the simulated recruiter (proposes times for applied cards, acknowledges bookings) run every 30 s by the `simulated-recruiter` job when `DEMO_FORMS_PORT` is set; `forms-server.ts` serves fixture Ashby, Greenhouse and Lever forms with a CAPTCHA fixture; `ports.ts` picks ports. Real tenants never reach any of it.
