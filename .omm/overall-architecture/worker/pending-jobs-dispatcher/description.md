`src/jobs/pending-jobs.ts`. Follow-on work is written to `pending_jobs` inside the transaction of the change that needs it (booked interview to `prep-plan`, offer to `negotiation`, simulated delivery to `mail-sync`). Every 30 s and at start the reconciler sends each undispatched row to the queue named by its `kind` with the row's `dedupeKey` as singleton key and stamps `dispatched_at`. A crash between send and stamp resends, which the singleton key collapses. It also repairs prep plans stuck pending with no owed work.
