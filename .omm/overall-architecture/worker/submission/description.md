`src/submission/`, job in `src/jobs/submit-application.ts`. Two attempts at a time per process, one browser, one context per application. `job.ts` takes an attempt lock, tailors the resume to a PDF, classifies the form, answers fields, fills and submits with a vendor adapter, runs the CAPTCHA chain when a widget appears, polls Gmail for a confirmation, records every step as activity and sets `applied` or `pending` with a `PendingReason`. A failure with a recorded outcome never reaches pg-boss; one without (database, profile load) is rethrown for retry.
