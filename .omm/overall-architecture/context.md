Hackathon MVP of a multi-tenant SaaS (PRD v1.1 in `docs/PRD.md`). Node 22+, TypeScript, pnpm workspaces, Vitest, Playwright. Behaviour was specified as five OpenSpec changes under `openspec/changes/` and built in four waves; `docs/STATUS-2026-11-07.md` records what is verified. Production is one DigitalOcean droplet running `deploy/docker-compose.yml`: postgres, a one-shot migrate container, api, worker and a Caddy container that serves the SPA and terminates TLS.
