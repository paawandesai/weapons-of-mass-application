What the system connects to and why. Read-only: the three ATS board APIs, Hacker News, LinkedIn public pages, GitHub, levels.fyi, Google Drive. Writes on the user's behalf: ATS application forms (and account creation), Gmail sends limited to scheduling, follow-ups, digests, reminders and CAPTCHA requests, Calendar events. The model is Anthropic only, through one client. For demo tenants a port factory swaps Gmail, Calendar and the ATS forms for local tables and a fixture forms server, so the demo never touches a real mailbox or vendor.
