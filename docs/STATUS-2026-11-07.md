## Decisions taken overnight (deviations from the OpenSpec designs)

- No second Gmail account for the simulated recruiter: demo tenants use a local mailbox and calendar behind the same ports the real Gmail and Calendar code uses (wave 4 brief, rule 12).
- A non-production demo sign-in gated by `DEMO_LOGIN_TOKEN`, because the fictional persona has no Google account (rule 13).
- Follow-on work (prep plan after booking, negotiation after offer, mail sync after simulated delivery) goes through a `pending_jobs` outbox written inside the parent transaction and dispatched by a reconciler, instead of enqueue-after-commit.
- Every outgoing email writes a pending delivery receipt before Gmail is called, so an uncertain send reconciles instead of repeating.
- Tests cannot reach the internet: a socket-level guard fails any test that tries.

3. Found by the third run: the web dev proxy did not forward `/confirm` and `/captcha`; the prep-plan
   schema's `maxItems` was rejected by structured outputs; and a flaky worker test was a real bug

## Success score (2026-11-07, 22:30 UTC)

`pnpm score [--tenant ID --user ID] [--since YYYY-MM-DD] [--json]` prints the one number defined in
`docs/design/success-score.md`: a gate (no money sent, no self-identification filled, nothing booked
without a confirm) that zeroes everything, then a weighted mean of five rates (submission 30,
discovery 20, answers 20, inbox 15, scheduling 15). Three of the five need a human label and print
as "unmeasured (N waiting)"; coverage says how much of the weight is measured.

Tonight's real account: score 56 at 30% coverage (submission 9 of 16 attempted; buckets:
unanswerable-question 4, unmapped-field 2, missing-profile-field 1; gate ok). The golden database
after the fourth run: 96 at 45% coverage (8 of 8 submitted, scheduling 7 of 8; the one miss is the
vague-time employer, declined on purpose). Scoring the earlier passing run found eight false
"no free slot" flags: the recruiter's own "Confirmed: Tuesday 2:00 PM" was read as a proposal of the
booked time (rule 57, fixed). The golden set stays a separate pass/fail line.
